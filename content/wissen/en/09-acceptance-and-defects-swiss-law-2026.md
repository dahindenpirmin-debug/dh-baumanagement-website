## Why acceptance matters so much

Three things happen at once on acceptance: the work is deemed delivered, risk passes to the owner, and the notice and limitation periods begin. A carefully prepared acceptance is therefore not a formality but the project’s most important quality moment.

## Procedure under SIA 118

1. **Notice of completion** by the contractor.
2. **Joint inspection** within one month, with a record.
3. **Outcome:**
   - no defects: work accepted;
   - minor defects: work accepted, deadline for rectification;
   - material defects: acceptance deferred, new inspection after rectification.
4. **Security:** the retention from interim payments is replaced by a guarantee for the duration of the notice period.

With separate trades, acceptance takes place trade by trade. Technical systems are additionally tested in operation: measurement records, integrated tests, instruction of operating staff.

## The periods at a glance

| | Statute (CO), contracts from 1 Jan 2026 | SIA Standard 118 (if agreed) |
|---|---|---|
| Notice of apparent defects | within 60 days of delivery | at any time during two years from acceptance |
| Notice of hidden defects | within 60 days of discovery | after the two years: within 60 days of discovery |
| Burden of proof | owner | on the contractor during the two years |
| Limitation | five years for immovable works | five years from acceptance |
| Deliberately concealed defects | ten years | ten years |

The 60-day period is mandatory: shorter periods in contracts or general terms are ineffective.

## What else the 2026 revision brings

- **Right to rectification cannot be excluded.** For buildings, the claim to rectification free of charge can no longer be contracted out. This also applies to the purchase of newly built property and affects the formerly common practice of referring buyers to assigned defect rights against the trades.
- **Contractors’ liens.** To discharge a lien, substitute security covering default interest for ten years is sufficient.

## Defect rights

Where there is a defect, the owner may under the Code of Obligations demand rectification, a price reduction or – for serious defects – rescission, plus damages in case of fault. SIA 118 first gives the contractor the right to remedy the defect within a reasonable period.

## Practice: giving notice properly

- **In writing and provable**, ideally by registered post or email with confirmation of receipt.
- **Specific:** location, component, appearance, date found, photos.
- **With a clear statement** that the contractor is held liable, and a deadline for rectification.
- **In time:** the period runs from discovery. Seeing damage and waiting puts your rights at risk.

## Before the warranty expires

We recommend a systematic inspection about three months before the two-year notice period ends, and another before the five years expire. Defects documented and notified by then remain secured; the guarantee is only released after rectification.
